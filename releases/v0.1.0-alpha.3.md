## Lesson flow and reference videos

- During phrase and three-star sentence practice, the signer video now loops on the sign you are being asked to perform. It advances with your answer and restores the correct clip after a retry or reload. The Dictionary phrase preview still plays the full sequence.
- Switching between course lessons now preserves each lesson’s prompt and position within a sentence. The practice screen shows the lesson name and prompt number.
- A qualifying course run now earns one stage star at a time, in word, phrase, and sentence order. Existing stars carry forward.
- Dictionary phrases return to the Dictionary and are tracked separately from saved-sign drills. Completed single-sign and saved-sign drills can be started again.

## Camera and scoring

- FSL practice and reference guides now use the FSL template when a sign such as Hello also has an ASL template.
- Self-review and Skip no longer write invented camera scores, XP, or telemetry. Skip queues the prompt for another try. Self-review completes the prompt, and the results screen identifies camera-free runs.
- Finger corrections now use the direction of the measured joint error, and the overlay highlights the hand used for the estimate. Angular-error telemetry reports the measured offset before the jitter allowance.
- Motion matches can advance on the frame that passes. Static letter drills reset an interrupted hold and keep the earned score visible after the hand leaves the frame.
- The lesson camera can be turned off during practice. The selected camera is remembered between launches.

## Window, dictionary, and desktop updates

- Small practice windows show a resize notice and keep the camera, reference, and controls reachable by scrolling. The desktop notice includes a **Maximize window** button.
- The Numbers filter includes 10. Changing filters clears out-of-filter results, and selecting a related sign opens its entry.
- Desktop update checks now use a signed-update manifest that includes alpha releases. **If you are upgrading from alpha 1 or alpha 2, download the Windows x64 installer from this release.**

Camera scores are estimates. Recognition accuracy with a live signer has yet to be validated.
